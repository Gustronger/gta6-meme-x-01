A GTA 6 meme for the [gta6-meme-x-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01): after a thirteen-year wait, one joyride down a Vice City sunset street earns five wanted stars in six seconds — and the make.fun ghost rides along as the sixth star. The meme is posted on X (its first picture is the entry); `main.ts` copies `POST.json` (the post's address on X) to `/output/POST.json`, where the gate reads it.
